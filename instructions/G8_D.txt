# G8_D — validation-measurement tooling and bounded smoke

## Purpose and scope

Implement codec-search, reconstruction-cache, BR-11 accounting, and clean-
classifier measurement tooling with atomic resume and bounded smoke only.

## Prerequisites and starting assertions

G8_C's complete hash-bound BLER table and immutable source manifest verify;
G8_D is next; no validation campaign or selection pass has executed; training
and test counters are zero.

## Exact outputs

Deterministic codec/reconstruction cache keys; emitted-byte-authoritative
feasibility; full BR-11 accounting; schema-bound per-candidate measurement
records; clean-classifier accuracy objects constructed from counts and
provenance; atomic campaign checkpoints; bounded fixtures/smoke; mutation tests
and updated handoffs. No full validation table.

## Checkpoint sequence

D0 verify/open; D1 freeze schemas/cache identity; D2 codec search and emitted-
byte feasibility; D3 reconstruction cache and BR-11 accounting; D4 count-
derived clean-classifier records; D5 atomic resume; D6 bounded smoke and
mutations; D7 full verification and handoff.

## Sudden-exit protocol

Record each intended cache/record work unit, outputs, exact first/restart
commands and counters before work; commit/push. Atomic outputs only; on resume,
verify hashes and continue the exact suffix.

## Prohibited work

No full validation campaign, pass one, training, artifact corpus, pass two,
ratio selection, fallback, test access, or later-phase command. Smoke must be
bounded and explicitly non-scientific. Later phases may not reinterpret cache
keys, byte semantics, schemas, or measured counts.

## Frozen-before-data decisions

G8_C BLER binding; candidate IDs; codec config/cache-key fields; emitted-byte
authority; BR-11 numerator/denominator semantics; record schemas; count-derived
accuracy provenance; work-unit order; and atomic-resume rules.

## Verification commands

Run targeted codec/cache/record/resume tests, bounded smoke verification,
mutation tests, all G8_A–C and upstream verifiers, docs/literals/spec checks,
full pytest, CPU-lock verification, and diff/status checks.

## Final report requirements

Report checkpoint SHAs, schemas and cache identity, accounting semantics,
smoke sample/cell counts, measured-object construction proof, atomicity/resume
proof, mutations, zero full-campaign/pass/training/test counters, all checks,
parity/cleanliness, and G8_E E0 as next action.
