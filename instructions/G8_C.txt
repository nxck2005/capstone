# G8_C — full-strength physical-layer BLER characterization

## Purpose and scope

Execute and freeze the complete full-strength BLER characterization grid fixed
by G8_A using the runner proven by G8_B. This phase creates physical-layer
evidence only.

## Prerequisites and starting assertions

G8_A and G8_B are green; campaign, identities, runner sources, seed policy and
state bindings verify; no identity or trial count drifted; characterization has
not started; validation pixels and test remain untouched.

## Exact outputs

Independent atomic work-unit artifacts at configured full-strength trials and
deterministic seeds; exact resume state; verified merge and complete coverage;
a separately hash-bound G-8 `BlerTable`; immutable characterization source
manifest; mutation evidence; updated ledger/handoffs.

## Checkpoint sequence

C0 verify/open; C1 mark and freeze execution sources; C2 execute resumable
shards; C3 merge independently; C4 verify every required identity and measured
SNR point; C5 build/freeze the G-8 `BlerTable`; C6 source-manifest and mutation
verification; C7 full verification and green handoff.

## Sudden-exit protocol

Before every shard or merge, ledger exact command, work-unit IDs, intended
paths, seed identities and restart command; commit/push the marker. Checkpoint
each completed unit atomically and record its hash. Resume only the exact
uncompleted set; never rerun or merge a unit silently.

## Prohibited work

No codec/reconstruction/classifier measurement; no validation pixels; no
ratio/operating-point selection; no pass one; no training; no G8_D+ command;
no interpolation outside measured support; no test access. Later phases may not
reinterpret the frozen table or merge schema.

## Frozen-before-data decisions

The complete identity grid, full-strength trial count, deterministic seeds,
decoder and SNR convention, work-unit boundaries, confidence/count fields,
merge order, completeness predicate, and G-8 table schema/source binding.

## Verification commands

Verify every unit hash/seed/trial count, exact resumption, one-to-one required
coverage, zero duplicates/omissions, no extrapolation, table loader/hash,
source manifest, mutations, G8_A/B contracts, upstream gates, full pytest,
CPU lock, and clean scope/status.

## Final report requirements

Report all checkpoint SHAs, work-unit and identity counts, completed IDs,
trial/error totals, seeds, merge/table/source hashes, complete-coverage proof,
no-extrapolation proof, zero validation/training/test counters, verification
results, parity, clean worktree, and G8_D D0 as next action.
