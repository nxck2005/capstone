# G8_F — artifact classifier and the single pass two

## Purpose and scope

Create the artifact corpus from training data only, fine-tune the configured
artifact classifier with checkpoint/resume and provenance, score validation
only after training, and execute pass two exactly once under a scorer identity
different from pass one.

## Prerequisites and starting assertions

G8_E is green; pass-one state and corpus specification are immutable and
verify; no training or pass two has occurred; test is sealed; the state is the
exact expected prefix.

AM-87 additionally governs corpus breadth after the post-pass-one/pre-F0 defect
audit. Before any F0 authorization, independently verify
`results/baseline/g8_f/corpus_plan.json` with
`tools/gen_g8_f_corpus_plan.py --check` and
`tools/verify_g8_f_corpus_plan.py`. The plan freezes the complete
authority-projected, exact-deduplicated artifact-quality request universe and
training-ID Cartesian attempt order. It is plan-only and is not itself F0
authorization. Owner audit of that plan is the next action. Do not materialize,
train, score, run pass two or start Pascal while the plan awaits audit.

## Exact outputs

A training-only artifact corpus and manifest; resumable training checkpoints,
logs, provenance and compute accounting; frozen artifact-finetuned checkpoint;
post-training validation scores; one immutable pass-two result; final pass-two
artifacts for G8_G.

## Checkpoint sequence

F0 verify/open; F1 materialize and verify training-only corpus; F2 freeze
training sources/config and preflight; F3 train with checkpoint/resume; F4
freeze and verify checkpoint/provenance/compute; F5 score validation; F6 run
pass two once with distinct scorer; F7 freeze pass-two outputs, mutations, full
verification and handoff.

## Sudden-exit protocol

Before corpus/training/scoring/pass-two work, record exact command, outputs,
restart command, completed units/epochs and hashes; commit/push. Resume training
from the exact verified checkpoint. Pass two uses an explicit pre-marker and
single immutable completion record; never retry it as a new pass.

## Prohibited work

No validation image enters training; no test access; no pass-one mutation; no
third pass; no ratio adjudication or fallback; no G8_G command. Validation
scoring begins only after training is frozen. Later phases may not reinterpret
corpus membership, scorer identity, checkpoint provenance, or pass-two state.

## Frozen-before-data decisions

G8_E corpus spec and pass one; AM-87's post-pass-one/pre-G8F disclosed corpus
projection, deduplication, order, multiplicity and infeasibility rule; training
split/breadth/recipe; checkpoint and resume identity; compute accounting;
post-training scoring boundary; pass-two scorer distinction; exact-once second
pass and two-pass termination.

## Verification commands

First verify the AM-87 metadata-only plan and its exact quality/train-ID order.
At F1, verify corpus split, complete attempt coverage, typed-infeasibility
omissions and absence of validation/test IDs or substitutions; then verify
checkpoint lineage and resume equivalence, compute/accounting records,
post-training validation ordering, distinct scorers, immutable pass one,
exactly one pass two and no third pass, mutations, prior gates, full pytest/CPU
lock, and clean status.

## Final report requirements

Report checkpoints, corpus counts/hash/split proof, training epochs/resumes/
compute/checkpoint hashes, scoring counts, pass-one immutability, pass-two
scorer/hash/selections, no-third-pass/test proof, all checks, parity/cleanliness,
and G8_G G0 next.
