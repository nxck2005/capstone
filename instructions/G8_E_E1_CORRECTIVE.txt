# G8_E E1 corrective pre-data supersession

This is an additive correction to the first G8_E E0/E1 freeze. It is a
pre-data contract repair, not a re-adjudication of measurements.

The original E1 artifacts and commits remain immutable history. Their status
is `superseded-before-data`, with zero validation coverage, because the first
E1 source epoch bound the refusal-only E2 runner and PB_3 smoke authorization
limits. The original E0 opening remains upstream history and is reused only
after its live assertions verify.

The corrected epoch freezes, before any owner-authorized E2 execution:

* the complete E2 transaction and production runner, including exact-prefix
  resume, immutable records/cache objects, locking, crash recovery, E3 merge,
  and E4 count-derived measured accuracy;
* the complete SNR-bearing logical authority and its exact mapping to an
  SNR-independent structural measurement authority;
* the exact JPEG2000 physical cache identity, including source pixels, payload
  budget, axis, codec configuration and runtime identity;
* the clean-measurement denominator and failure semantics from BR-4/BR-5/BR-6/
  BR-11/BR-13, with the separately measured outage term never folded into
  `acc_clean`; and
* the intended pass-one call grouping and the typed `G8Authorization` limits.

No authorization artifact is issued by this correction. The corrected runner
may authenticate the frozen contract and assets, but `--start` and `--resume`
must refuse before validation payload decoding while owner E2 authorization is
absent. Synthetic tests are explicitly non-scientific, non-selection fixtures
and are not eligible for production merge.

No G8_C or G8_D science is changed. No E2 measurement, pass one, training,
fallback, ratio adjudication or test access is permitted during this epoch.
