## Campaign-wide frozen decisions

Before data, the campaign freezes the W4 adjudication bytes, its independently
verified `selection_policy_sha256`, the exact selection-source bytes, the
specification and generated parameters, dataset split-manifest bytes, the
ordered phase list above, exact-float tie equality, the six-field tie-break
order, `params.baseline.core_modulation` as the fixed-modulation reference, the
two-pass sequence and termination rule, the complete `BlerIdentity`, the
configured structural axes, and validation-only selection with the test split
sealed. Changing any bound scientific policy after campaign start invalidates
the campaign.

Later phases may consume and extend earlier artifacts only through their
declared schemas and hashes. They may not silently reinterpret, regenerate
away, weaken, broaden, sort into validity, or relabel an earlier artifact. A
schema or policy contradiction is a HOLD, not permission to infer a convenient
meaning from observed data.

## Sudden-exit protocol

Before a checkpoint expected to exceed a few minutes, mark it `in-progress` in
`instructions/RESUME.md`; record the exact first command, intended files and
outputs, and restart command; commit; push; and confirm local HEAD equals
`origin/main`. At completion, run targeted verification, record exact results,
mark it `done`, record the exact next command, create a non-empty checkpoint
commit, push, and confirm parity. Never hold more than one coherent checkpoint
uncommitted. Never amend, squash, rebase, or force-push.

**Pascal successor custody exception (AM-86).** The paragraph above remains the
default for every phase and for the historical local G8_C campaign. For the
owner-authorized `confessor_pascal_cu126` successor only, its crash-atomic,
campaign-bound per-unit request/result/state evidence may accumulate
continuously in the separate mutable runtime on `confessor` while the dual-GPU
campaign runs unattended. Git publication may occur after that invocation exits
or at an owner-selected manual checkpoint; loss of evidence not yet published is
an explicitly accepted owner custody risk. A manual checkpoint or final G8_C
handoff still performs verify → reconcile → commit → authenticated HTTPS push →
fetch/parity, and complete publication/parity is mandatory before BLER-table
freeze or G8_D release. This is publication timing only: no other writer, host
switch, old-result ingest, evidence weakening or later-phase access is allowed.

If signing fails, preserve the staged tree and intended subject, record the
failure and exact retry command in the ledger, and retry that subject when
signing works. Do not replace it with a placeholder subject.

When capacity is uncertain, preserve ledger status, changed files, known tests,
completed work-unit IDs, artifact hashes, and the exact next command, then
commit and push before reporting an incomplete verdict.

## Campaign-wide prohibitions

No phase may access test before G-12. No phase may extrapolate G-2 BLER evidence,
silently treat missing BLER as zero, weaken the baseline, change scientific
parameters without an amendment, or reinterpret an earlier artifact. Only the
phase explicitly assigned characterization, validation measurement, training,
selection, or adjudication may perform it. A later-phase command must never be
run early.

## Common final report and verdict rules

Every phase reports its start and end SHAs, parity, worktree state, checkpoint
SHAs, created/changed files, artifact hashes and counts, exact verification
results, prohibited-work proof, and exact next action. Each child instruction
defines its terminal `GREEN`, `INCOMPLETE`, and `HOLD` strings. `GREEN` means
only that phase's outputs exist and verify; it never implies later scientific
work has happened.
