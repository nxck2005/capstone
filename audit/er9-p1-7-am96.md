## Existing normative rules retained

The following ER-9 rules were already normative before AM-96 and were not
reinterpreted: the task-aware digital-control role; the shared_with_learned and
differs_only_in contracts; the configured real-valued dimension and bit grids;
uniform_scalar quantisation and the configured STE; static_range_coder;
offline train-only entropy fitting; no learned entropy model; endpoint table
installation with table bytes excluded from per-image payload; the same classical
digital physical layer and transport-tuning contract; matched-k accounting; the
one-bit raw/range selector; raw-escape sizing; validation-only selection; and
the sealed test boundary. These are found in the ER-9 and G-11 requirements,
the digital_semantic_control parameter block, and AM-55, AM-58 and AM-59.

## AM-96-only executable closure

AM-96 supplies the previously missing concrete tap, task-only loss, dimension
factorisation, operation and serialization order, per-ratio scope, quantizer
range/scale/clipping, exact staged search lists and tie rules, staged count
semantics, search/production seed relationship, and exact range-branch framing.
The corresponding machine-checkable freeze artifact binds this audit by its
canonical-byte SHA-256 and rejects any ER-9 scientific artifact that predates
the freeze.
