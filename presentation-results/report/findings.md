# W10 scientific findings (validation only)

## Reading the measurements

Each point has **1,000 Imagenette-160 validation images** from the single frozen train/channel seed cell. End-to-end accuracy is `n_correct / 1000` and includes outcomes on transmission outage. Delivery coverage is `(1000 − decode failures − infeasible transmissions) / 1000`. Accuracy conditional on delivery uses only delivered images and is undefined when coverage is zero. The `10.0%` end-to-end floor seen on several all-outage digital points is the measured outage-policy result on this exactly stratified validation set; it is **not** successful delivery. The per-image row correctness, including outage decisions, is the source of each aggregate `n_correct`.

Bandwidth ratios are channel uses relative to the source representation: `1/6` uses **12,800** symbols per Imagenette-160 image, and `1/24` uses **3,200**. These are matched channel-use budgets within each ratio, not matched transmitted information or feature formats. Full-grid SNRs in dB are −8 through +7 in one-dB steps, then 9, 11, 13, 15 and 18. All figures place them at their actual numeric positions.

The learned systems use their respective **own task heads**. The adaptive classical/JPEG/fixed controls are primarily scored by the **artifact-finetuned reference classifier**; the same transmitted outputs also have a `clean` classifier stream in `w10_scorers_357.csv`. The ER-9 task-aware digital arm uses its own task head on a separately trained feature representation. The reconstruction ablation uses the frozen G-1 clean classifier. Thus a learned-versus-classical accuracy difference combines communication, representation and scorer effects. Comparisons among classical variants at one ratio share the artifact-finetuned scorer. The fixed-SNR, randomized and PAPR learned runs use the same *kind* of task head, but distinct trained weights.

## Results

1. **Graceful low-SNR degradation.** At 1/6, standard learned DJSCC goes from **72.8% at −8 dB** to **79.4% at −4 dB**, then reaches **83.4% at +18 dB**. It delivers a task prediction at every tested SNR. These are descriptive outcomes from one frozen seed cell, not a population robustness interval.

2. **The adaptive digital system wins after it can deliver.** At 1/6, adaptive classical has zero delivery and **10.0%** end-to-end accuracy at −8 through −5 dB. At −4 dB, coverage rises to **100%** and accuracy to **83.4%**, above learned's **79.4%**. It exceeds the standard learned curve at every measured SNR from −4 to +18 dB, reaching **89.3%** versus **83.4%** at +18 dB. The sampled transition is between −5 and −4 dB; no threshold between those points was measured. Coverage dips at −2 dB (97.7%) and +9 dB (96.2%), so the digital curve is not strictly monotone.

3. **SNR-randomized training improves the observed weak-channel region.** At 1/6 it records **77.0% versus 72.8%** at −8 dB, **82.4% versus 79.4%** at −4 dB, and **83.9% versus 83.4%** at +18 dB (randomized versus fixed-SNR). It is higher at most measured points, with small crossings/near-ties at stronger SNRs. The distinct checkpoints and one seed cell prevent a claim about average training-seed improvement.

4. **A fourfold tighter bandwidth budget shifts the low-SNR tradeoff.** Standard learned DJSCC falls from **72.8% to 49.4%** at −8 dB when moving 1/6 → 1/24, but from **83.4% to 82.8%** at +18 dB. At 1/24, adaptive classical has no delivery through −3 dB; at −2 dB it reaches only **31.0%** end-to-end, and at 0 dB **69.4%** versus learned's **78.7%**. It first exceeds learned at the measured **+4 dB** point, yet the two curves cross again locally around the +9 dB coverage dip. Do not claim a single monotone crossover for this ratio.

5. **Task-aware digital coding is a useful control.** The ER-9 arm uses the same **1/6 channel-use budget**, a separately trained task-aware feature representation, and its own task head. Its end-to-end accuracy is **10.0%** through −5 dB, then **82.0%** at every measured point from −4 to +18 dB. Standard learned DJSCC retains accuracy below the delivery transition and reaches **83.4%** at +18 dB. The control shows that digital transmission of task-aware features can be strong when it delivers; the gap at low SNR involves outage behavior as well as representation and training. It does **not** isolate a channel-code effect.

6. **The PAPR-constrained variant is compliant and competitive in this validation cell.** Its symbol-domain maximum is **3.000002 dB**, within the frozen **3.0 dB + 0.0001 dB tolerance**, versus **20.059284 dB** maximum for the unconstrained learned system. Its mean PAPR is **3.000001 dB** versus **13.218188 dB**. At −8 dB it scores **75.9% versus 72.8%**; at +18 dB **83.2% versus 83.4%**. The constrained run is a separately trained checkpoint, so its pointwise differences are not solely a causal PAPR penalty. PAPR is measured on channel symbols, **not** an oversampled, pulse-shaped RF waveform.

7. **Secondary controls explain mechanisms and scope.** At 1/6, the fixed MCS control remains at the outage-policy floor until +6 dB and then reaches 89.0%; fixed modulation begins delivering later than adaptive selection and plateaus around 88.7% at high SNR. JPEG-secondary reaches **88.6%** at +18 dB but has a real **0% coverage / 10.0% accuracy** point at +9 dB; keep this unfavorable measurement visible. The predicted-label control stays at **82.0%** once delivery succeeds. This is a transmitted *predicted* label, not an oracle true-label result. Reconstruction followed by the frozen clean classifier scores **44.7%** at −8 dB and **76.8%** at +18 dB, below direct task-head scoring; because the scorer changes, this is a pathway ablation rather than an isolated measure of reconstruction quality.

## Limits that matter in a viva or paper

- These are **validation-only** results from one frozen seed cell and 1,000 images per point. The final test is sealed. Do not call the 21 SNRs independent experimental replications or infer significance from smoothness of the curves.
- The published per-image stream manifest is authenticated, but the bulk rows are not in this checkout. No paired uncertainty intervals were computed here. Point differences of one or two percentage points should be described as observed differences.
- The headline learned and classical systems use different classifier architectures and task representations. Their end-to-end curves answer a system-level question, not a controlled comparison of channel coding alone.
- The classical chain has coding, codec and artifact-aware scoring choices frozen from validation work. The learned controls use separate training runs. This is a concrete, specified comparison, not equal optimization effort or equal computational complexity.
- Outage fallback, finite validation size and frozen discrete operating choices produce abrupt steps and occasional nonmonotone points. No measurements were inserted between published SNR values.
- Symbol-domain PAPR does not establish amplifier or over-the-air compliance. The project remains simulation-first; hardware behavior is future work.
